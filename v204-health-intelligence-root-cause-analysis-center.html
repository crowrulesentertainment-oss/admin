Hypothesis States

UNTESTED

A possible factor has been recorded but not evaluated against evidence.

SUPPORTED

Available evidence is consistent with the hypothesis.

CONTRADICTED

Available evidence conflicts with the hypothesis.

INCONCLUSIVE

Evidence is insufficient to distinguish the hypothesis.

Root Cause Review

OBSERVATION What was actually recorded?

TIMELINE What happened before, during, and after the event?

DEPENDENCY Which systems or components were demonstrably connected?

ALTERNATIVES What other explanations fit the same evidence?

COUNTEREVIDENCE What observations weaken the hypothesis?

CONFIDENCE Is the evidence direct, supported, contextual, or unknown?

Critical Guardrail

ROOT CAUSE IS NOT ASSUMED
V204 does not label a suspected cause as a fact merely because events are close in time or share a service. A root-cause conclusion requires documented evidence, consideration of alternatives, and human review.

Safety & Platform State

V204 is an investigation and decision-support surface. It does not trigger recovery, send notifications, alter routing, delete records, or expose service-role credentials. No new Edge Function is deployed; the existing function-capacity limit remains respected.
