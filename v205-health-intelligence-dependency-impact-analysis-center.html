Dependency Types

DIRECT

A documented integration or explicit data relationship.

SHARED DATA

Multiple surfaces rely on the same authorized data domain.

OPERATIONAL

One service depends on another service's operational availability.

CONTEXTUAL

A relationship is useful for investigation but has not been proven as a runtime dependency.

Impact States

OBSERVED

An affected behavior or metric has been directly recorded.

SUPPORTED

Multiple pieces of evidence support the impact relationship.

POSSIBLE

The dependency or propagation path is plausible but unconfirmed.

UNKNOWN

Available evidence cannot establish an impact relationship.

Recommended fields: impact_id, incident_id, source_service_key, affected_service_key, dependency_type, impact_state, scope_start, scope_end, evidence_ids, observed_effect, propagation_path, confidence_state, reviewed_by, reviewed_at, created_at, metadata.

Critical Guardrail

IMPACT IS EVIDENCE-BOUND
A service being nearby in the architecture does not establish that it was affected. V205 distinguishes documented dependencies, observed impact, plausible propagation, and unknown relationships. Human review is required before an impact assessment is treated as an operational conclusion.

Safety & Platform State

V205 is an analysis and decision-support surface. It does not trigger recovery, send notifications, change routing, modify service configuration, delete records, or expose service-role credentials. No new Edge Function is deployed; the current project function-capacity limit remains respected.
