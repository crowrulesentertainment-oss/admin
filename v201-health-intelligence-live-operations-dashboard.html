Freshness & Evidence

TIMESTAMP Every live metric should carry its observation time.

SOURCE Each panel should identify its source table or server-side endpoint.

STALE Old telemetry should be visibly distinguished from current observations.

UNKNOWN Missing data should remain unknown rather than being converted into a healthy state.
