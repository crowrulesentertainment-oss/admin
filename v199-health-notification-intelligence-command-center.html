Intelligence Guardrails

Evidence: every signal should identify its source metrics and comparison windows.

Inconclusive: insufficient samples must not be represented as confirmed anomalies.

Human control: intelligence informs operators and does not make production decisions.

Data minimization: analytics should use aggregate metadata wherever possible and avoid unnecessary notification content.
