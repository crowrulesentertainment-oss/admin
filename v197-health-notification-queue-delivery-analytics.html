Analytics Dimensions

By service: identify services generating the most operational communications.

By severity: compare watch, degraded, critical, and recovery traffic.

By channel: compare dashboard, email, webhook, and future channels.

By template: identify the most frequently rendered notification types.

By time: inspect hourly/daily queue depth, latency, retries, and failures.

Operational Alerts

QUEUE GROWTH Detect sustained increases in pending work.

LATENCY Detect delivery delays beyond an approved threshold.

RETRY SPIKE Detect unusual repeated attempts.

FAILURE SPIKE Detect elevated terminal or provider failures.

DUPLICATES Detect unexpected duplicate-generation patterns.

Safety & Privacy

ANALYTICS WITHOUT PUBLIC PRIVILEGE
V197 defines metrics and reporting architecture without exposing notification payloads or privileged credentials to the browser. Analytics should favor aggregate metadata and avoid retaining unnecessary message content or sensitive recipient information.

Automation: OFF by default · Delivery: trusted server-side only · Edge Functions: no new deployment while capacity is exhausted.
