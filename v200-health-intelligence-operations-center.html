V200 Guardrails

Human control: intelligence provides evidence and context; operators remain responsible for production decisions.

Security: service-role credentials stay server-side and protected tables remain behind authorization boundaries.

Reliability: retries, cooldowns, deduplication, and bounded execution prevent uncontrolled loops.

Privacy: analytics should favor aggregate metadata and minimize notification content and recipient information.

Platform: current Edge Function capacity is respected; no bypass or unauthorized deployment path is introduced.
