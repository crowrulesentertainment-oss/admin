Graph Investigation

UPSTREAM Identify documented sources that may influence the selected service.

DOWNSTREAM Identify surfaces that may consume or reflect a selected capability.

PATH Trace a bounded sequence between two explicitly related nodes.

IMPACT Connect only evidence-supported affected surfaces.

TIMELINE Compare graph relationships with observed event timing.

AUDIT Preserve the evidence used to establish or reject a relationship.

Graph Record Contract

Recommended fields: edge_id, source_service_key, target_service_key, edge_type, confidence_state, evidence_ids, valid_from, valid_until, observed_at, description, reviewed_by, reviewed_at, created_at, metadata.

Critical Guardrail

GRAPH ≠ PROOF OF CAUSATION
A node being connected to another node does not establish that one caused an incident or that downstream services were affected. V206 keeps dependency structure, evidence, and impact conclusions separate.

Safety & Platform State

V206 is an analysis and visualization surface. It does not change service configuration, trigger recovery, send notifications, delete records, or expose service-role credentials. No new Edge Function is deployed; the current project function-capacity limit remains respected.
