Explainable Insights

Evidence first: every insight should identify the metric, comparison window, dimensions, and underlying event counts.

No hidden scoring: operational signals remain traceable to observable telemetry.

Human review: insights inform operators; they do not automatically change production policy.

Confidence context: insufficient sample sizes should be marked as inconclusive rather than treated as a confirmed pattern.

Suggested Intelligence Records

signal_key · signal_type · service_key · severity · metric · baseline_window · observed_window · observed_value · baseline_value · delta · sample_size · confidence_state · explanation · created_at · metadata

These fields describe an analytics model; V198 does not create an autonomous decision engine.

Safety State

DECISION SUPPORT — NOT AUTONOMOUS CONTROL
V198 adds explainable analytics architecture. It does not automatically escalate incidents, modify routing, send notifications, delete data, or execute recovery actions. Privileged operations remain server-side and no new Edge Function is deployed while project capacity is exhausted.
